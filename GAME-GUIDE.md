# NEON CLASH — Comprehensive Game Guide

> Covers `neon_clash_4.2.html` (latest). Older versioned files (`3.27`–`4.1`) are prior builds; always play the highest number.

Neon Clash is a 2D neon cyber-brawler for browser: pick a fighter, gear up, and fight bots or friends across 17 arenas, 18+ game modes, with clans, trading, quests, and full progression.

Settings → Controls: click any of the 12 actions × 2 slots, press a replacement (Esc cancels). Stock defaults always work alongside remaps. Reset restores defaults. Gamepad uses default keys.

## 3. Combat mechanics

- **Guard (hold E):** blocks 60% damage from the front. Perfect-tap timing = **parry** (no damage, attacker frozen, projectiles reflected).
- **Guard-break meter:** blocking builds wear (blue→red bar under your HP). Max it and guard shatters — full damage + 1.5s freeze. Meter decays when not guarding.
- **Stun = frozen state.** Sources: guard-break, Chrono Lock accessory (3s), Frostbite Round / frost socket / Frost kit, perfect parry (attacker only). Roots hold position but don't stop attacks.
- **Ultimate (U at 100%):** per-fighter super, charged by dealing damage.
- **Team combos:** two different teammates hitting one foe within ~0.8s = +25% damage + popup.
- **Assists (tag mode, V):** benched partner fires their special from your position. 20s cooldown.
- **Hazards:** lava/acid/toxic/ice/stormcore/mines per map; toggleable in match setup.
- **KOTH:** hold the gold zone; capture slowed 3× vs old builds.
- **Stock:** 3 lives, respawn with invuln; **Juggernaut:** you're 3×HP vs 3 hunters, survive 75s; **Hoard:** 60s, hits shake coins loose, richest wins (capped conversion); **Infection:** random patient-zero, tagged fighters turn, survive 90s.
- **Pickups:** health/quad/ult/speed/spring/**beam blade** (3 empowered 1.8× melee hits, then breaks).
- **Kill feed** (top-right), **combo ranks**, **damage vignette** on hurt, **training DPS meter** (H panel, rolling 5s).

## 4. Progression per fighter

- **Upgrades:** 4 tracks × 5 levels (HP, damage, special, cooldown), coins per level.
- **Skill tree:** 6–8 nodes in 4–5 tiers with requirements, coin costs.
- **Prestige:** max everything → reset for +5% stats/level, cost scales 1k × (level+1). Prestige aura ring (gold→purple→pink→cyan) in fights and previews.
- **Skins:** equip per fighter (shop, themed auto-sets for every fighter incl. new ones, custom editor skins).
- **Accessory / Armor / Companion / Relic:** one slot each, equipped per fighter tab.
- **Socket:** one signature-move modifier (Volatile, Siphon, Heavy, Swift, Frost, Venom, Echo, Guardian, Surge).
- **Emote loadout:** 3 slots (keys 1–3), assigned in the Emotes tab from owned emotes.
- **Intro tab:** entrance pose (None/Jump/Spin/Dash, plays every round, AI too) + custom victory line on the KO splash.
- **One-time weapons:** see §6.

## 6. One-time weapons (Armory)

6 weapons (Pipe Bomb, Frostbite Round, Overclock Serum, EMP Grenade, Cluster Missiles, Juggernaut Serum), coins, bought in bulk packs. Equip **per fighter** (multiple fighters can hold weapons at once — cards show the `On:` list) via Armory Equip buttons or the Accessory-tab strip. Fire with **I** (priority over accessory); each shot consumes stock, auto-unloads at zero. HUD **W** slot shows loaded weapon + live stock. Player-only (AI doesn't use them); stock is local-only.

## 12. Tips

1. Break turtles: guard-break > raw damage. Perfect parries cost no meter.
2. Remap keys before ranked-style grinds; defaults always work as fallback.
3. Buy weapons ×10, equip across 2–3 mains, fire early — stock is tempo.
4. Hoard: fast multi-hit wins; cap is 150 — bank it and play safe after.
5. Infection: stay airborne-adjacent to crowds; zombies cluster.
6. Juggernaut: you are the raid boss — corner control beats chasing.
7. Spin + first-win bonus daily; prestige scales, so time it after maxing.
8. Replays double as scouting: watch how bots punish you.
